# GAIN Credit

> GAIN Credit is a market-leading fintech providing responsible, AI-powered credit solutions for people underserved by traditional banks and lenders. It operates three consumer lending brands — Lending Stream, Drafty, and Afforda — primarily in the UK, with operations across the US and India.
